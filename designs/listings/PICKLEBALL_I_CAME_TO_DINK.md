## Why this layout (research → design)

Winning pickleball joke tees on Etsy / Amazon / specialty shops cluster into:
1. **Bold stacked type + paddle/ball** (Crazy Dog club look, “dinking problem” sellers)
2. **Swagger mascot / character** with short punchline (matches our live Soft Serve that already listed)
3. **Vintage badge / crossed paddles** (TeePublic “I Came I Saw I Dinked” lane — crowded)

We shipped **#2**: Soft Serve–grade sticker mascot + huge lime **DINK** so the joke reads at thumbnail size on dark shirts. Crossed-paddle badge and text-only PSA styles were tested and not chosen (badge is flooded; sparse text+ball underperformed last attempt).

**Do not clone:** Soft Serve pickle vegetable, Dawn frog, TeePublic laurel crest, WitWear layouts.
