## Print / export notes

- Export uses **`--threshold 235`** to remove warm off-white AI background without car surgery.
- **Do not** run defringe/halo scripts on the master.
- Chrome/silver wheels required in generation — they carry the thumbnail read on white shirts.

---

## QC checklist

- Correct **C5** body — fixed exposed headlights, long hood fastback (NOT pop-up C4 headlights)
- **Factory Black** paint with visible silver edge highlights on hood/fenders
- **Debadged smooth nose** — no AI badge slop
- **Chrome/silver Z06 wheels** — NOT black wheels (needed for contrast)
- **Solid V8** — no horizontal strikethrough
- 4-line stagger quote via overlay (matches collection workflow)
- Pure **#FFFFFF** background
- No front plate / plate pocket
- Dark tinted glass — no checkerboard windshield
