# Car Culture Wave 1 — 3 type-first tees (brand-free)

**Status:** **READY FOR UPLOAD** (Sep 21, 2026) — art + Printify exports done, nothing live yet
**Why these three:** they reuse the only non-Corvette format that has ever converted for us (type-first insider joke, like **IT WAS IN**), they target the buyer we already rank for (car enthusiast gift), and they contain **no brand wordmarks, logos, or model names** — so they carry none of the Corvette takedown risk.
**Why no OpenAI spend:** composed locally with Pillow + Anton. Regenerate any time with `python3 scripts/compose_type_first_tee.py`.

**Rules that apply to all three**
- **Dark shirts only** — Black, Navy, Charcoal / Dark Heather. The type is white + accent; it disappears on light shirts.
- **Ads OFF for the first 14 days.** These are gate tests (see `NICHE_PORTFOLIO.md`), and an ad fee on a $22 tee is what killed Yellow C5 margin.
- **One SKU per niche** until that niche produces a sale. Do not add a second manual/garage/autocross design before then.
- **Printify scale:** these are wide-and-short text blocks. Set the print ~11–12 in wide and chest-placed (~3 in below collar) instead of accepting the default scale, or it will look small on the mockup.
- **Pricing:** eBay S–XL **$21.99**, 2XL **$23.99**, 3XL **$24.99** · Etsy S–XL **$22.99+**. Gildan 64000 runs ~$14–16 all-in at Printify, so this clears the **$5+/unit** floor without a coupon.
