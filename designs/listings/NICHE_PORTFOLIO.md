# CozyOrbitPrints — Niche Portfolio Plan (Sep 21, 2026)

Goal from owner: **~10 niches, each producing a sale now and then**, so revenue is consistent and no single niche can zero out a month. This file is the plan for getting there. Read with `SHOP_STATE.md`.

---

## What our own numbers already proved

| Niche | SKUs listed | Sales | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corvette apparel | ~15+ | ~20 | The entire business. Also the entire IP risk. |
| Posters (C5 Blueprint) | 1 | 1 (Sep 20) | Best margin in the shop — keeps ~43% of order total |
| Pickleball | 4 | 1 | Only the **type-first** joke sold. Both AI-illustrated designs: 0. |
| Meme tees (text-to-image) | several | 0 | Low views, no clicks — taken down Aug/Sep |
| Kawaii / stickers | — | 0 | Abandoned |

**The pattern that sells is not "Corvette."** It is: *art that names the buyer's specific tribe.* `C4`, `C5`, `C6`, `C7` are identity badges — a C6 owner buys the C6 shirt. `IT WAS IN` is the same thing in words: only pickleball players say it. C4 Minimal was thin text with barely any illustration and still had our best ad ROAS.

**The meme shirts failed because a generic joke names no tribe.** Anyone could wear it, so nobody searched for it and nobody felt seen by it. That is a targeting failure, not an art-quality failure — which matters, because it means better AI art would not have saved them.

**So the 10 niches should each be a tribe with its own vocabulary**, not a style or a vibe.

### One diagnostic correction worth keeping

"Little views and no clicks" are two different diseases and they need different fixes:

- **Few views/impressions** → tags, title, and price competitiveness. The design was never actually shown, so you learned *nothing* about the art. Rewrite title + tags once before killing it.
- **Views but no clicks (CTR under ~0.5%)** → thumbnail/mockup or the design itself. This is the real "art didn't work" signal.
- **Clicks but no sales** → price, mockup set, shipping, or lack of reviews.

Before ending any remaining listing, check which of the three it is. Killing a zero-view listing teaches us nothing and may throw away art that simply had bad tags.

## The gate (the discipline that was missing)

Every new niche launches as **one** SKU and has to earn the second one.

| Stage | Rule |
|---|---|
| Launch | 1 SKU, dark shirts, type-first, **ads OFF**, record the launch date |
| Day 14 | Read views → clicks → sales, in that order, using the diagnostic above |
| Kill | Under ~0.5% CTR on 200+ views → end the listing, keep the file, log the result |
| Fix once | Under ~50 views → one title/tag rewrite, then re-read at day 28 |
| Expand | 1 organic sale **or** CTR above ~1.5% → build SKU #2 in that niche only |
| Hard cap | Max **3 live SKUs per niche** until the niche has 2+ sales |

That cap is the rule that would have stopped pickleball at 1–2 SKUs instead of 4, and would have caught the meme tees before a whole batch went live.

**Why waves of 3 and not all 10 at once:** our observed hit rate on brand-new art is roughly 1 in 5. Ten simultaneous launches would likely yield 1–3 working niches while burning attention and leaving thumbnails we can't easily fix later (see the eBay note below). Waves let each launch teach the next.

## Margin reality per format

| Format | Revenue | Printify | Net (organic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Type-first tee @ $21.99 | ~$22 | ~$14–16 | **~$4–7** |
| Poster 20×30 @ $32 | $32.03 | $14.76 | **~$13.78** |

Tees are the cheap way to *test* a niche. Posters and hoodies are how a proven niche actually pays. So: test with tees, then move winners into posters/hoodies rather than adding a fourth tee.

## Corvette: de-risk, do not abandon

It is ~90% of revenue; walking away would be the expensive mistake. The takedown was specific — the **CORVETTE wordmark inside the artwork**.

- No brand wordmarks, model names, or crossed-flag logos **in the art**. Chassis code + silhouette + spec quote only — that system already sells.
- Do not relist the struck art on Etsy. eBay is still selling it; fulfill those orders, but expect eBay VeRO to be at least as aggressive, so keep a safer replacement ready.
- Diversification is the actual insurance policy here: if a strike lands while Corvette is 90% of revenue, the month is gone.

## Practical note: the eBay thumbnail you couldn't change

If those listings were published to eBay **through Printify**, Printify owns the images — editing on eBay directly is blocked or gets overwritten on the next sync. Change the mockup in the Printify product editor and re-publish/update the listing. If eBay still won't take the new primary image, ending the listing and republishing from Printify is usually faster, and it also clears a stale listing's poor click history. (Verify in Printify before assuming — this is the likely cause, not a confirmed one.)
