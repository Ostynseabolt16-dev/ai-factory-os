## How to run them

Generators misspell type. Use the **no-text** prompt, then set the words yourself in Anton (same font as IT WAS IN and Dinking Problem). If you use a model that can spell, the all-in-one prompt is below each one. Reject any result with a misspelled word, a logo, or holes in the paddle.

## 1. Kitchen enforcer — the mascot

The shirt you already like. One change: the paddle face is solid. Holes belong on the ball. A paddle full of holes reads as a second ball, and the scratches make that worse, not more vintage. Distress stays on the hat, the badge, and the lettering.

**No-text prompt**

```
A centered chest graphic for a t-shirt, isolated on a pure transparent background, no mockup, no shirt, no mannequin, no scenery. Vintage screen-printed mascot, bold black outlines, flat colors, slight distressed ink speckle on the print so it looks worn in, not muddy.

One round lime pickleball character, about #D6EF1F, with black circular holes only on the ball. Confident smirk, one raised eyebrow, small black eyes. Wearing a puffy white chef's toque with a black band and light scratch distress in the white. The character's right hand (viewer's left) is a white glove held up in a clear stop gesture, palm forward, four fingers and a thumb. The character's other hand grips a pickleball paddle.

The paddle is the important part: a normal solid-face paddle, elongated oval head, short throat, wrapped handle. The face is the same lime as the ball but completely solid, with a thin dark edge guard around the rim and two or three light scratch marks. No holes, no perforations, no dots, no mesh, no waffle pattern, no second ball on a stick. The grip is white and black. The paddle is large enough to read as a paddle from across a room.

Under the character, a rectangular lime yard-sign badge with a dark outline and two small bolts, empty, so type can be added later. Leave clear space above the chef hat for an arched line of type, and clear space under the badge for one small line of type. Do not draw any letters, numbers, or words anywhere.

No logos, no brand names, no crossed paddles, no real paddle brand shapes, no people, no kitchen background, no photorealism, no 3D plastic look.
```

**Type to set after, in this order**

- Arched over the hat, lime with a dark outline: `STAY OUT OF MY KITCHEN`
- Inside the badge, heavy black: `NO ENTRY`
- Under the badge, smaller white: `NON-VOLLEY ZONE ENFORCER`

**All-in-one prompt, only if the model can spell**

```
Same graphic as above, and render these exact words with perfect spelling, no extra letters: arched over the hat in lime with a dark outline, STAY OUT OF MY KITCHEN. Inside the lime badge in heavy black, NO ENTRY. Centered under the badge in smaller white letters, NON-VOLLEY ZONE ENFORCER. If any word is misspelled, leave all text off.
```

## Do not generate

- Holes on the paddle
- Corvette, Chevrolet, Stingray, or any car badge in the same image
- Crossed paddles, a beer mug, or a letter replaced by a ball
- A copy of a specific shirt you saw on TikTok or another Etsy shop
- Selkirk, JOOLA, Wilson, or any paddle logo
